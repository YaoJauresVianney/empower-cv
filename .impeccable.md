## Design Context

### Users
HR managers at small and mid-size companies (SMBs). They use Empower CV daily, running active hiring campaigns — posting job offers, reviewing candidates, generating AI shortlists, and scheduling interviews. They are not necessarily technical, but they are efficient and goal-oriented. They want to get through their tasks quickly without cognitive overhead. The interface should support repeated, high-frequency workflows without fatigue.

### Brand Personality
**Confident · Efficient · Professional**

The product should feel serious and trustworthy — like a tool that gets out of the way and lets the recruiter work. Not flashy. Not playful. Not over-designed. Every element should earn its place by helping the user accomplish something. The AI features should feel capable and reliable, not gimmicky.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light mode primary. Clean white content areas, deep purple (`#4f0067`) as the brand anchor, soft purple-tinted backgrounds (`#f4f2f7`).
- **Typography**: Manrope — geometric, professional, legible at small sizes. Maintain tight tracking for headings, comfortable line-height for body.
- **Visual tone**: Refined data-dense layout — think Bloomberg or Linear, not Notion or Figma. Information is primary; decoration is minimal and intentional.
- **Icons**: Material Symbols (outlined weight). Consistent size discipline.
- **Motion**: Subtle fade-slide entrances (`animate-in`). No bouncing, no elastic. State transitions only.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards with teal/gradient headers, hero metrics with gradient text, glassmorphism panels, card-grid repetition without hierarchy variation.

### Design Principles
1. **Data first** — Content and numbers are the hero. UI chrome should recede; let the information breathe.
2. **Consistency over creativity** — Reuse patterns rigorously. Tokens, spacing, and component anatomy should be predictable across every page.
3. **Earn every element** — If a visual element doesn't help the user understand or act, remove it.
4. **Confidence through contrast** — Deep purple on white is intentional and bold. Don't dilute it with too many accent colors.
5. **WCAG AA compliance** — All text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, all interactive elements keyboard-accessible with visible focus states.
